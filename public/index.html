Second shop (no GST)

Bills for this shop have no GSTIN, HSN or GST, are numbered separately, and sync with the main shop. Switch shops by tapping the shop name at the top left. The signature below is used for both shops.

Finance companies (EMI)

Choose one on a bill under Payment → Finance / EMI. Its narration template is written into the bill's narration with the bill's figures filled in. You can use {finance} {emiDay} {downPayment} {financeAmount} {total} {customer}, and ______ for details to write on each bill (loan no., EMI amount).

Signature on bill

Use a PNG with a transparent background. A photo of a signature on plain white paper also works: the white is removed automatically. The signature is printed above "Authorised Signatory" on every bill, print-out and PDF.

Backup & restore

Restoring replaces every bill, product and setting in the place you're currently saving to (this device, or your synced shop when signed in).
